## What the round delivers

Acceptance for H09.01 — "deterministic bracket for N>=2 models" — is met by a pure bracket data structure plus its match contracts:

- `buildBracket(models)` seeds models in the given order (seed 1 = first entry) into a standard single-elimination layout sized to the next power of two. Slot order uses the classic construction `order(2s) = for v of order(s): [v, 2s-1-v]`, so each first-round pair sums to `size - 1` (seed 1 meets seed N, seed 2 meets seed N-1 …) and leftover slots become byes that always fall to the better seeds. Round-0 byes resolve at build time (status `bye`, winner fixed) and cascade into resolved round-1 players.
- Match contracts: every match carries a positional id `r<round>m<slot>`, exactly two player slots, feeder ids, a winner, and a status of `pending | ready | played | bye`. `readyMatches` lists playable matches; `recordResult` is the only mutation path.
- `recordResult(bracket, matchId, winner)` is pure: it returns a fresh bracket, validates the match exists, is `ready`, and that the winner is one of its two players (structured throws otherwise), then propagates the winner into the next round's feeding slot. `bracketComplete`, `bracketChampion` (winner of the final) and `bracketRanking` (deepest round reached, then seed) close out the read side.

## Contract invariants satisfied

- **Determinism**: no I/O, no clock, no randomness. Equal model lists produce deep-equal brackets (asserted), match ids and pairings are a pure function of the input, and replaying the same results in the same order reproduces the identical bracket (asserted).
- **N >= 2 with byes**: rejects `< 2` models, empty names and duplicates with structured errors; for every N from 2 to 33 the round count is `ceil(log2 N)` and a bye never meets a bye (asserted in a sweep).
- **Immutability**: `recordResult` never mutates its input (asserted via JSON snapshot); all transitions are copy-on-write.
- **Valid transitions only**: recording on an unknown match, a non-player winner, or a not-ready/already-decided match throws before any state changes (asserted).
