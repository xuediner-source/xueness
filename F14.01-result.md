## Contract Invariants Satisfied
- **Real build and key-link evidence per platform**: a cell can only be `accepted` when its probe carries a `BuildEvidence` with `exitCode === 0`, at least one artifact, `ranOn` equal to the cell platform, and at least one passing `KeyLinkEvidence`; validators reject accepted cells lacking any of these (also on file load).
- **No extrapolation across platforms**: evidence produced on another platform forces `not_accepted` with a "cross-platform evidence is not acceptance" reason (directly encodes "当前已安装版证据主要是 Windows，不能外推三平台" — the Windows-installed evidence cannot promote Linux/macOS cells).
- **Unexecuted items are explicitly not accepted**: absent probes, missing build evidence, or an explicit blocker produce `not_run` with a concrete blocker string; `not_accepted` cells always carry reasons.
- **Completeness**: the matrix must contain exactly the 12 platform×entry cells; missing or duplicate cells fail validation and persistence.
- **Counts, never rates**: summaries carry only `{accepted, not_accepted, not_run}` counts; any rate/coverage/percent/progress field anywhere in the document is rejected by `validateEntryAcceptance` (mirrors the F00.04 acceptance-harness "样本数不是成功率" rule).
