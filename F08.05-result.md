# F08.05 Result — 完善父子生命周期和清理 (parent-child lifecycle & cleanup)

- **Status**: Completed
- **Files changed this round**:
  - `ts/plugins/subagents/src/lifecycle.ts` (new): the F08.05 lifecycle core.
    - `TERMINATION_REASONS` (parent_stop / task_cancel / budget_exhausted / child_error / crash_recovery / shutdown_timeout) with a durable, Python-shaped `TerminationRecord` (`reason` + 500-char `detail` + `at`), guarded by `isTermination`/`terminationReason`.
    - `TaskLeases`: exclusive write leases with generations — a resource is held by at most one task; re-acquire by the holder is idempotent, a release/expiry hands over with `generation + 1`, TTL renews only for the holder (`renew`), `releaseTask` frees everything one task holds, `revokeExpired` is the cross-task dead-holder sweep, `onRelease` fires on every release path, and a `clock()` hook makes expiry testable.
    - `TaskBudget`: turn budget in abstract units — dispatch reserves up front (`DEFAULT_TASK_RESERVE` = 8), `settle` replaces the reservation with the child's real consumption on any terminal outcome (success, failure or cancel all attribute), `overBudget` gates new dispatch, and `attribution()` reports which child spent what (reserved vs charged).
    - `terminateTask`: the single terminal funnel — status flip, then the diagnosable termination record (clean success leaves none; every abnormal terminal leaves one), then lease release — so no terminal path can keep a lease or lose the reason.
    - `recoverLifecycles`: cross-task recovery — every registry row still `running` without a live worker is terminated as `crash_recovery` (leases and budget settle through the same funnel) and expired leases of dead holders are revoked, returning a `RecoveryReport`.
  - `ts/plugins/subagents/src/coordinator.ts`: wired onto the funnel.
    - `attachLifecycle({leases, budget})` attaches the F08.05 machinery (both default to "not enforced"); `budget()`/`leases()` expose it.
    - `dispatch`/`resumeChild` reserve budget up front (refusal creates no row and leaks no slot), the child's stop-poll becomes `stopped() || registry.isCancelled(tid)` so a targeted cancel is observed at the child's next safe boundary, and every terminal branch funnels through `terminateTask` with the right reason (`parent_stop`, `task_cancel`, or `child_error` on failure) plus budget settlement.
    - `cancelTask(taskId, detail)`: targeted cancellation of exactly one of this parent's children — validates ownership and id shape, answers `already_terminal` for finished tasks, terminates through the funnel, wakes waiters, and returns the retained `termination` plus `released_leases`.
    - Constructor recovery and `sync()` now terminate leftover/parent-stopped children with `crash_recovery`/`parent_stop` reasons; bounded shutdown diagnoses grace-period overruns as `shutdown_timeout`; a throwing worker body is terminated as `child_error`; and the `#start` `finally` is belt-and-braces — whatever path a worker took, its leases are released and its budget entry settled. `termination` joined the coordinator's durable `DURABLE_FIELDS`.
  - `ts/plugins/subagents/src/background-tools.ts`: new `task_cancel` tool (`CANCEL_TOOL_NAME`) joining `BACKGROUND_TOOL_NAMES` (now four coordinator-gated tools); it rejects unbound coordinators and settles through `coordinator.cancelTask`.
  - `ts/plugins/subagents/src/task-registry.ts`: `termination` added to the fields `restore()` copies from journal/saved rows, so a reason survives registry reconstruction.
  - `ts/plugins/subagents/src/index.ts`: re-exports `./lifecycle.ts`.
  - `ts/plugins/subagents/test/lifecycle.test.ts` (new, 23 tests) and `ts/plugins/subagents/test/lifecycle-coordinator.test.ts` (new, 6 tests).
